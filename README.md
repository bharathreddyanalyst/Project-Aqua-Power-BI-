# Project-Aqua-PowerBI
This is a Power BI end-end Project

=> Aqua Company – Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

Developed a multi-page Power BI Business Intelligence solution analysing 2023–2024 sales performance, customer behaviour, and product trends to support executive decision-making. The project integrates interactive dashboards, KPI tracking, and advanced DAX calculations for business insights across revenue, customers, products, and regional performance.

1. Home Dashboard

Created a navigation-driven landing dashboard connecting Sales Overview, Customer Performance, and Product Performance reports for streamlined business reporting and user accessibility.

2.Sales Overview Dashboard

A.Analysed £102.63M total sales with 6.84% Year-on-Year growth using KPI cards and trend analytics.
B.Built monthly sales trend visualisations identifying peak revenue periods, with highest sales exceeding £9.4M.
C.Evaluated top-performing countries and customers, highlighting France as the leading sales region at £18M revenue.
D. Analysed Incoterms contribution, identifying 79.15% sales concentration under “Carriage Paid” transactions.


2. Customer Performance Dashboard
   
A.Monitored 3.18K customers and calculated £32.23K average revenue per customer using DAX measures.
B.Identified top 5 customers contributing 62.24% of total revenue, supporting customer segmentation analysis.
C.Built country-wise customer distribution and growth analysis dashboards to evaluate regional customer profitability.
D.Developed Year-on-Year growth trend analysis showing customer growth exceeding 1189% by December.

3.Product Performance Dashboard

A.Analysed 60 product categories generating £102.63M total product sales with 6.84% YOY product growth.
B.Calculated £1.71M average revenue per product using advanced Power BI measures and KPI modelling.
C.Identified highest-performing products, with Product Hierarchy 2002 generating approximately £8.9M revenue.
D.Built declining product analysis visuals to identify underperforming products and support revenue optimisation decisions.
